Lotta Little Lists

This is David Duffey's collection of lists of things and people in the works of Gilbert & Sullivan.

This list was created by David Duffey and was subsequently maintained by scynthius@aol.com. (This is one of those fascinating Internet relationships. Even though we have never met, and have no expectation that we ever will, we have worked together to create an interesting site.) It is now being hosted and maybe maintained by Chris Angelico. All credit to the aforementioned; all I am doing is keeping it alive.
